# first word of the expected-behaviour column): colloq = roundabout / colloquial phrasing of a recorded thing; neg = negated or hedged yes/no; typo = typing and speech-to-text damage; tele = telegraphic keywords;
# thres = numeric limits and units; negcond = "not charging", "outside the city"; jargon = engineer vocabulary; trend = frequency over time and comparisons of events; abstain = must NOT be executed;

# why = causes; spec = fixed facts and where a reading comes from.  ADV-v2 (sealed) is written in the same families with different sentences.  Rows from this half may be read to find failing FAMILIES; training
# rows are written from the families, never from these sentences (the builder's leakage filter, --eval-leak v8, drops any row at Jaccard >= 0.8).
