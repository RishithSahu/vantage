## 1.3.4 - 2026-10-25 (petrol and diesel in fuel words; hardening round: one start-up, no caches, calibrated confidence, route policy, sealed records, test corpus, release manifest)
* **Per-trip fuel**: "compare fuel consumption across my last five trips" / "each of my last 4 trips" give litres per 100 km for every trip (from the fuel-rate and speed minutes of that trip), not litres per hour.
* **Hour of the day** for fuel: "which hour of the day has the highest average fuel consumption" - litres per 100 km by clock hour (hours with under 5 km of driving are left out), from the same minute tables.
* **Two named periods** in a fuel comparison: "before and after my last service" and "this week with last week" now compare the two periods asked (the older is the base), not the first period against "the one before it".
* **"fall below"** is no longer read as the season (autumn): "when did my tyre pressure last fall below the recommended threshold" reads the stored pressures.
* **Electric words put to a fuel car** are read as the matching fuel quantity and the first line says so: state of charge / battery level at a time, at the start and end of a trip, lowest / trend (= fuel level); battery health = the 12 V battery; "how long to charge" / "how long to fill the tank" = the litres still needed (and what your last refuels added) - no pump time is invented.
* **Trip estimates in fuel words and either word order**: "how much fuel will 50 km use", "how many litres for 100 km", "can I drive 400 km" (a yes or no against the tank).
* Tests: `tests/test_owner_round2_ice.py` (11 checks on each of the petrol and the diesel car, against separate SQL).

### 1.3.4 - hardening round (the review table: start-up, caches, confidence, queue, routes, records, test data, provenance)
* **One start-up** (`vgpt/bootstrap.py`): `vehiclegpt.py run`, `python ui/server.py` and `app.py` harden the process, adopt `models/product.json` (adapter, frame 2, ONNX Runtime build), keep the memory guard and run the doctor in the same way; starting the interface module directly no longer bypasses any of it. `/api/health` reports the model that answers (adapter file + SHA-256, ONNX build hash, frame version, backend, decoder) and the release.
* **No answer cache, no frame cache**: the 2-second answer cache of the interface and `VGPT_FRAME_CACHE` are gone. Only the SAME request (session + request id) is joined while it is running; the next question with the same words is a new run. (`tests/test_no_cache.py`)
* **The worker**: bounded queue (a full queue is "busy" at once, HTTP 503), a deadline per job (a job nobody waits for is never run; one that was running has its effect on the conversation undone), a conversation per session, request ids. (`tests/test_worker_queue.py`)
* **Confidence**: the reported confidence is the calibrated share of development questions read right at that score (no floor of 0.5, no ceiling of 0.95); below 0.50 an answer is withheld ("I will not guess") instead of being given as certain: on the check half of 2,989 development questions this withholds 2.7 % and catches 17 % of the wrong readings (`vgpt/meaning/calibration.py`, `bench/calibrate_confidence.py`; `VGPT_MEANING_REFUSE_BELOW`). None of the owner's real questions is withheld. Both backends read the score over the tokens the decoder may allow.
* **Decoder**: one bounded decode (the longest valid frame), a confidence over the allowed tokens only, no whole-vocabulary scan, no retry with the grammar sampler (a decode that is not a frame fails closed), a dead mask state fails closed; ONNX Runtime does not busy-wait unless there are cores to spare.
* **Camera**: no decoding or analysis unless the picture is on screen or a fault is injected.
* **Routes**: one table, deny by default (`vgpt/routepolicy.py`); simulation controls never in `--production`, never for the fleet role, never while driving with the lock on; the study pages only with `--research` and the participant's consent; the raw feedback export only for the service and validation roles.
* **Records**: feedback and study lines are cut short, sealed (AES-GCM), removed after 30 / 14 days and erasable (`vgpt/recordstore.py`). The fleet summary under an answer is keyed by what the history contains, not by a 60-second clock.
* **Honest boundary**: `docs/BOUNDARY.md` lists every stage as learned or code; `VGPT_PREPARSER=0` / `VGPT_MODEL_ONLY=1` switch the pre-parser off for a measurement of the model alone. The product no longer calls itself model-only.
* **Test data and provenance**: `tests/testdata.py` + `tools_testdata.py` (groups, hashes, absent data skips with the reason and FAILS in the release gate); a small versioned corpus `tests/corpus/` (electric, petrol, diesel, a synthetic hybrid, J1939 / OBD-II / proprietary CAN, missing signals, injected faults, stale data, no data); a derived driving fixture so that the world generator and its tests run without the real traces; Hypothesis-free module load; the live-model comparison runs with the product's own adapter; `tools_release_manifest.py` writes `RELEASE.json` (source digest, models, vocabulary, test data, runtime, git state, installer hash) which the doctor verifies; `VantageCli version` now prints 1.3.4.
* Found by the corpus and fixed: an impossible tyre pressure (-700 kPa on the bus) is reported as a faulty sensor, not as a flat tyre; a hybrid whose profile says plug-in hybrid keeps its battery vocabulary; `make_meaning_brain` no longer demands an adapter file when a model is handed in.
* Tests: `test_bootstrap`, `test_route_policy`, `test_worker_queue`, `test_recordstore`, `test_confidence`, `test_no_cache`, `test_fleet_cache`, `test_release_manifest`, `test_release_model`, `test_corpus`, `test_boundary`, `test_version`.

* **Time, fixed in the parser**: "last week" / "last month" are the calendar week / month before this one ("the last week", "the past week", "the last 7 days" stay rolling); "till now", "up to now", "to date" mean the whole history; "3 sundays ago", "two fridays ago" and "the 11th" are read; a history that ends exactly at midnight ends the day before, so "today" is never an empty day; far-back periods are named by dates.
