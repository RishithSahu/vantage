What is real in this demo

Real: question understanding (rules + SLM), SQLite analytics, camera analyser (numpy only), ingest of camera metadata into the same store the assistant queries, event thumbnails.
Stand-ins: video decoding (OpenCV on the laptop), telemetry replayed from the simulated 28-day history (NOT synchronised with the video), speech recognition (see Test bench), the lane-assist indicator (derived from camera state, not a real ADAS function).
