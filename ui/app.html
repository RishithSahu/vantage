Before you start

VehicleGPT answers questions about your vehicle's recorded data. It is an advisory assistant, not a safety system: it cannot see the car live, cannot control anything, and is no substitute for a workshop, the owner's manual or your own judgement.

  • Everything runs on this computer; the assistant makes no internet connections.
  • The data shown here is a simulated vehicle history for demonstration.
  • The microphone button uses your browser's speech recognition (Chrome and Edge); depending on the browser that may send your voice to its vendor. Typing never leaves the computer.
  • The thumbs up / down buttons save your rating on this computer only.
